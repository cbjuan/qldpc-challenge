\begin{abstract}
We report progress in a continuous automated search over periodic rectangular
bivariate-bicycle (BB) quantum LDPC codes.  The run is orchestrated by OpenAI
GPT-5.6 Sol through the Codex harness and follows the Unitary Foundation qLDPC
Challenge's tool-agnostic autoresearch protocol (``autoresearch mode'' here):
generate reproducible constructions, screen exact
structural properties, persist every logical witness, attack promoted
candidates at increasing randomized-search budgets, and let the repository's
trusted validator---rather than the agent---decide whether a result is a
campaign find.  The campaign may be viewed as a continuation of our earlier
LLM-guided BB-code search, with a different harness and repository trust
gate~\cite{cruzbenito2026}.  At the stated data cutoff, 47,163 structural
proposals have produced 45 board-advancing local finds representing 44
distinct parameter triples.
Two distinct \qcode{696}{236}{3} constructions jointly maximize the campaign
score $\score=k^2\dw/n$ at $240.07$.  The largest witness-supported distance
label among the finds is $d\leq9$, attained by six constructions.  All
distance statements remain upper bounds and all novelty statements remain
provisional.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, terminology, and status}

The construction family is locked to the periodic abelian group
$G=\mathbb{Z}_{\ell}\times\mathbb{Z}_m$.  Open-boundary, twisted-torus,
non-abelian generalized-bicycle, coset, lifted-product, and balanced-product
codes are out of scope.  Work remains on the
\pathcode{autoresearch/2026-08-06-local} branch of the personal fork
\url{https://github.com/cbjuan/qldpc-challenge}.  Campaign artifacts may be
archived on that branch, but no candidate has been written to \pathcode{codes/},
submitted to the challenge, or used to open a pull request.
The surrounding verification and leaderboard framework is described in the
QEC Challenge whitepaper~\cite{qecwhitepaper}; the current public board is the
challenge website~\cite{qecwebsite}.

\paragraph{Data cutoff and inclusion rule.}
This is a frozen working-tree snapshot at \textbf{2026-08-07 16:02:20 CEST},
based on repository commit \pathcode{ef7f13b} plus local artifacts already

all 45 are included.  Candidate files without a trusted verdict are excluded
from counts, rankings, and extrema regardless of their screening weight or

The campaign uses the following deliberately narrow meaning of a
\emph{find}: the saved call to \pathcode{verify/validate_candidate.py} returns
\pathcode{passed: true}, and its novelty gate returns
\pathcode{board_advancing: true}.  ``Board-advancing'' is relative only to the
repository snapshot and its computed Pareto cells.  It is not a claim of
literature novelty or of global state-of-the-art performance.

For compactness, this report writes a campaign label as \qcode{n}{k}{\dw}.
Here $\dw$ is the lightest logical weight persisted by the campaign, so the
mathematical statement is
\[
  d_X\leq w_X,\qquad d_Z\leq w_Z,\qquad
  d\leq \dw:=\min(w_X,w_Z).
\]
Thus \qcode{n}{k}{\dw} must not be read as an exact-distance certificate.
The same caveat applies to every score containing $\dw$.

\section{Construction and exact structural screening}

Let
\[
  R=\GF[x,y]/(x^{\ell}-1,\,y^m-1),
\]
and let $A,B\in R$ be arbitrary binary sums of monomials, with unequal support
sizes allowed.  Their regular representations are $\ell m\times\ell m$
binary circulant matrices.  The CSS checks are
\[
  \HX=\begin{bmatrix}A&B\end{bmatrix},\qquad
  \HZ=\begin{bmatrix}B^{\mathsf T}&A^{\mathsf T}\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Because $A$ and $B$ commute and the arithmetic has characteristic two,
$\HX\HZ^{\mathsf T}=AB+BA=0$.  The resulting block length and encoded
dimension are recomputed exactly as
\[
  n=2\ell m,
  \qquad
  k=n-\operatorname{rank}_{\GF}(\HX)
      -\operatorname{rank}_{\GF}(\HZ).
\]
The search respects the repository bounds $n\leq700$ and total check weight
at most 32.

Before any distance work, the campaign performs exact GF(2) rank and CSS
checks, canonicalizes translations and axis symmetries, removes construction
duplicates, records connected-component structure, and rejects a known
translated-$A=B$ trap that forces a weight-two logical whenever the code
encodes.  This distance-free census makes the expensive witness search a
promotion step rather than a property of every proposal.

\section{Automation and research workflow}

The two automation layers have different responsibilities.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Codex harness.}  GPT-5.6 Sol operates as a repository-aware
  research agent: it inspects the current board and field notes, implements
  and invokes the deterministic campaign runner, monitors artifacts, adjusts
  allocation according to observed collapses, and maintains the decision
  journal.  Shell execution, file persistence, and source-control awareness
  make long multi-rung experiments resumable and auditable.

  \item \textbf{Unitary Foundation autoresearch protocol (``autoresearch
  mode'').}  The repository's tool-agnostic autoresearch manual supplies the
  scientific control loop and trust boundary.  The model may propose, rank,
  and test constructions, but it cannot declare its own result valid.  Every
  find must be packaged by the research kit, preserve its witnesses, and pass
  the pinned validator.  The trusted \pathcode{verify/} stack is never
  modified by the campaign.
\end{itemize}

The implemented loop is
\begin{center}
\small
\fbox{proposal} $\longrightarrow$ \fbox{exact structural screen}
$\longrightarrow$ \fbox{diverse selection}
$\longrightarrow$ \fbox{persisted witness}\\[0.45em]
$\lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow$ \fbox{RIS ladder}
$\longrightarrow$ \fbox{trusted gate}
$\longrightarrow$ \fbox{local ledger}.
\end{center}

Proposal IDs and random seeds are deterministic.  The initial lanes cover
weight-4 breadth, high-rate weight-6 and weight-8 gaps, arbitrary unequal
supports, axis-mixed supports, shared-factor motifs, and mutations of
reproducible BB seeds.  Later phases add targeted nearby-geometry mutations,
controlled-order common-factor transfers, and a high-distance continuation
around parents carrying reported bounds $d\leq8$, $d\leq9$, $d\leq14$, or
$d\leq18$.  A parent's reported distance label is a selection prior only:
every child obtains and persists its own witness.

The key operational safeguard is persistence-first execution.
\pathcode{submit.make_submission} packages an initial Python result; every
deeper accelerated rung saves both raw witness evidence and a new candidate
file without overwriting its ancestor.  Accelerated witnesses are then
rechecked by the trusted Python GF(2) predicates.  Consequently, an expensive
logical operator is not lost if a later run or save fails.

The pending exact tier formulates a binary mixed-integer linear program for
each logical sector and each complementary logical-basis generator.  It asks
whether a nontrivial logical of weight $1,\ldots,\dw-1$ exists while enforcing
the parity-check constraints and an odd logical overlap.  Infeasibility of
every such problem on both sectors certifies the persisted upper bound as the
exact distance; a feasible solution refutes it; a timeout leaves the result
at upper-bound confidence.  The first MILP queue should prioritize the six
$d\leq9$ finds and the six $d\leq8$ finds, while retaining all timeout and
counterexample artifacts.

\subsection{In-flight candidates excluded from the results}

At the data cutoff, 64 additional packaged candidates remained in three
active survivor queues without any saved

screen.  None is a campaign find, and none contributes to the inventory,
distributions, rankings, or extrema above.  Their persisted logical weights
are screening upper bounds only and may decrease under deeper attacks; the

lanes, not exact-distance certification.  Later artifacts written after the
cutoff are deferred to a subsequent report revision.

\section{Limitations and immediate next steps}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{External reproduction.}  Rebuild the highlighted candidates
  from their monomial supports in a clean checkout and on an independent
  machine, rerun fresh-seed witness searches, and compare file hashes and
  validator verdicts.  This is the immediate next validation step.

  \item \textbf{Exact MILP certification.}  Run the SciPy/HiGHS certifier on
  both logical sectors, beginning with the $d\leq9$ and $d\leq8$ finds.  Save
  feasible counterexamples, logged infeasibility outcomes, and all timeouts;
  do not relabel a distance exact unless
  the authoritative certifier returns \pathcode{d_exact: true}.

  \item \textbf{Independent distance corroboration.}  Run the BP+OSD
  residual-witness method with fresh seeds.  Agreement would be valuable
  cross-mechanism evidence, but would still not supply a lower bound.

  \item \textbf{State-of-the-art and equivalence review.}  No systematic
  comparison with the current literature, preprints, or independent code
  tables has begun.  Search for equivalent BB support presentations and
  qubit permutations, compare against published parameter families, and
  retain the label ``literature novelty unverified'' until that review is
  complete.

  \item \textbf{Human promotion decision.}  Only after reproduction,
  certification/corroboration, and literature review should a human decide
  whether any staged JSON belongs in \pathcode{codes/}.  This report itself
  authorizes no submission.
\end{enumerate}

The numerical source of record for the inventory is
\pathcode{FINDINGS.md} together with the adjacent candidate and
