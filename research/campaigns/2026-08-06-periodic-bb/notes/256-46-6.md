## Dead ends

Although it advances the current public board, this code is locally dominated
by `[[238,46,6]]`, which has the same k and witnessed distance with 18 fewer
qubits. It is retained as a calibration result, not as a preferred local
frontier candidate. The reconstructed `[[288,50,8]]` parent was also rejected
as an exact board duplicate.
