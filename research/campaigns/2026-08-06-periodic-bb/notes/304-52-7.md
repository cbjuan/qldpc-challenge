# [[304,52,7]] — high-rate weight-8 periodic BB

## Direction & hypothesis

Search nearby periodic-BB geometries around the known `[[288,50,8]]`
construction, prioritizing useful `d>=3` codes with high `k^2*d/n`.

## What was searched

20,000 broad deterministic proposals followed by 20,000 targeted parent
mutations. Candidate 20058 is a nearby-geometry transfer to a `19x8` torus
with the parent's 4+4 supports.

## Evidence trail

The witnessed distance stayed at 7 across 400, 8k, 60k, and the required 1M
accelerated rung for `n>=300`. The final rung used 8,000 Python trials and
1,000,000 accelerated trials at seed 1887582743, persisting a weight-7 X
witness. Trusted validation at seed 1440004648 passed, independently found
nothing lighter, found no exact or WL-equivalent board entry, and labels it as
advancing weight-8 x unrestricted.

Claim: `d <= 7`, not exact; literature novelty unverified.

- Campaign priority: `k^2*d/n = 52^2*7/304 = 62.2632`.
- Board FOM: `k*d^2/n = 52*7^2/304 = 8.3816`.

## Dead ends

No distance collapse occurred through 1M. The original `18x8` parent
reconstruction was rejected separately as an exact board duplicate.

## Tools

Periodic BB kit, deterministic campaign runner, `gf2_fast`, trusted Python
witness checks, and the local trusted validator.
