# [[696,232,3]] — maximal-size order-3 weight-6 periodic BB

## Direction & hypothesis

Scale the useful order-three shared-factor branch to the largest periodic BB
geometry allowed by the repository schema, while keeping check weight 6 and a
witnessed distance above two. This directly targets the campaign's additional
`k^2*d/n` metric without leaving the periodic rectangular-torus family.

The construction is maximal in block length for this order-three mechanism.
A factor generated by an element of order three requires `3 | l*m`, while the
schema imposes `l*m <= 350` because `n = 2*l*m`. The largest admissible multiple
of three is 348, realized here as `3*116`, so this lane can reach `n=696` but
not 700.
