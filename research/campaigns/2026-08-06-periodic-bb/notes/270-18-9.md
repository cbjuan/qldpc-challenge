# [[270,18,9]] — balanced weight-6 periodic BB

## Direction & hypothesis

Search balanced, connected periodic BB geometries for distance as well as rate,
after the first high-rate factor kernels collapsed to `d=2–4`.

## What was searched

20,000 deterministic arbitrary-support proposals followed by a balanced
24-code witness batch. This hybrid-kernel candidate uses a `9x15` torus and
unequal 4+2 supports.

## Evidence trail

The witnessed distance stayed at 9 across the saved 2k, 8k, and 60k ladder.
The final rung combined 8,000 Python trials with 60,000 accelerated trials at
seed 852276664; the raw weight-9 X witness passed the Python GF(2) checks.
Trusted validation at seed 2092693552 passed and independently found nothing
lighter in 8,000 trials. The validator labels it as advancing weight-6 ×
unrestricted. Claim: `d <= 9`, not exact; literature novelty unverified.

FOM: `k*d^2/n = 18*81/270 = 5.40`.
