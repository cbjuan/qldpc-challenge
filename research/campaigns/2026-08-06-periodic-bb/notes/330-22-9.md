# [[330,22,9]] — high-distance weight-6 periodic BB

## Direction & hypothesis

Search the periodic rectangular-torus bivariate-bicycle family with witnessed
distance at least 8 as a hard promotion priority, while seeking a useful
encoded-dimension sweet spot. This candidate transfers the support of the
local `[[270,18,9]]` result from `9x15` to an `11x15` torus.

## What was searched

The campaign screened deterministic targeted mutations of witnessed and known
periodic-BB parents. Candidate 20283 is a connected, indecomposable nearby-
geometry proposal with 4+2 polynomial supports. It was packaged at 400 Python
RIS trials and promoted through saved 8k, 60k, and 1M accelerated rungs. The
60k and 1M rungs reused the persisted Python-8k witness rather than rerunning a
Python search whose result could be lost.

## Evidence trail

The witnessed distance stayed at 9 through the complete 400→8k→60k→1M
ladder. The final accelerated rung used seed 694493808, pair depth 16, four
threads, and persisted a weight-9 X witness. Trusted validation passed,
independently found no lighter logical in 8,000 RIS trials (seed 466015227),
found no exact or WL-equivalent board entry, and labels the code as advancing
weight-6 x unrestricted.

Claim: `d <= 9`, not exact; literature novelty unverified.

- Campaign priority: `k^2*d/n = 22^2*9/330 = 13.2000`.
- Board FOM: `k*d^2/n = 22*9^2/330 = 5.4000`.

## Dead ends

high-distance candidates promoted alongside it passed the trusted gate but did
not advance their board cell.
