# [[272,48,8]] — high-rate weight-8 periodic BB

## Direction & hypothesis

Search the periodic rectangular-torus bivariate-bicycle family for useful
high-rate codes, prioritizing `k^2*d/n` while rejecting `d=2` candidates as
correction-code leaders. This candidate is a nearby-geometry mutation of the
known `[[288,50,8]]` periodic BB construction.

## What was searched

20,000 deterministic arbitrary-support proposals were followed by 20,000
targeted mutations of witnessed and known periodic-BB parents. Candidate 20118
uses a `17x8` torus with 4+4 supports. It was first packaged at 400 Python RIS
trials, then promoted through saved 8k and 60k rungs.

## Evidence trail

The witnessed distance stayed at 8 across 400, Python/accelerated 8k, and
Python-8k/accelerated-60k searches. The final accelerated rung used seed
1707877945 and persisted a weight-8 X witness; both final X and Z witnesses
have weight 8. Trusted validation at seed 59706361 passed, independently found
nothing lighter in 8,000 RIS trials, found no exact or WL-equivalent board
entry, and labels the code as advancing weight-8 x unrestricted.

Claim: `d <= 8`, not exact; literature novelty unverified.

- Campaign priority: `k^2*d/n = 48^2*8/272 = 67.7647`.
- Board FOM: `k*d^2/n = 48*8^2/272 = 11.2941`.

## Dead ends

The parent geometry was also reconstructed as `[[288,50,8]]`, but the trusted
gate identified that candidate as an exact duplicate of the existing board
entry. No distance collapse occurred for candidate 20118 through 60k.

## Tools

Periodic BB kit, deterministic campaign runner, `gf2_fast`, trusted Python
witness checks, and the local trusted validator.
