block-length limit on a torus with both dimensions substantial. The earlier

deep confirmation at `d <= 4`; candidate 40007 tests whether the quarter-rate

## Dead ends

No collapse occurred across the confirmation ladder. Direct large-geometry
transfers of an order-4 motif can lose rate when the factor displacement's
order changes with the torus; this construction fixes the factor at order 4
and retains rate one quarter. Relative to `[[688,172,4]]`, it saves eight
physical qubits while encoding two fewer logical qubits at the same witnessed
distance and check weight, so neither continuation strictly replaces the
other on the local frontier.
