# [[696,236,3]] — order-3 high-rate periodic BB transfer

## Direction & hypothesis

Extend the validated `[[396,136,3]]` shared-factor BB code along its long torus
axis while preserving the common factor's order. The campaign prioritizes
`k^2*d/n` in addition to the board's `k*d^2/n`; holding the `Z_6` axis and the
4+4 supports fixed was expected to retain the one-third-rate, order-3 branch
up to the schema limit.

## What was searched

A structural transfer pass considered periodic rectangular-BB continuations
with `l*m <= 350`, using exact k, connected-component, and board-fingerprint
checks before any witness work. Candidate 40000 is the explicit held-support
transfer from `Z_6 x Z_33` to `Z_6 x Z_58`. It has exact `k=236`, check weight
8, and one Tanner component of size 696. It was packaged at 400 Python RIS
trials and promoted through independently seeded 8k, 60k, and 1M rungs.

- Campaign priority: `k^2*d/n = 236^2*3/696 = 240.0690`.
- Board FOM: `k*d^2/n = 236*3^2/696 = 3.0517`.

## Dead ends

The score gain comes from rate, not increased distance: the board FOM remains
modest and the four-rung ladder consistently found weight 3. Lower-order
factor mutations have larger projected `k^2*d/n` but are natural distance-
collapse risks; they were not substituted for this order-preserving transfer.
Direct transfers of the separate order-4 `[[384,96,4]]` parent also lose its
rate when the diagonal factor's order changes with the torus geometry.

## Reproduction

On `Z_6 x Z_58`, use
`A={(0,0),(1,25),(3,25),(4,0)}` and
`B={(0,0),(1,12),(3,12),(4,0)}` with
`research/kit/bb.py::build_bb`.

Both blocks share `f=1+x^2`, whose monomial displacement `x^2` has order 3 on
`Z_6`. Explicitly,
`A=f*(1+x^2+x*y^25)` and `B=f*(1+x^2+x*y^12)`. Enlarging only the y-axis
therefore preserves the factor orbit length. For this held-support motif,
`k=4m+4*gcd(m,13,50)`; at `m=58` the gcd is 1, giving `k=236`.
